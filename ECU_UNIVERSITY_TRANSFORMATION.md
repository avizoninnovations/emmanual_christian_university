## 1. Institutional Profile

### About Emmanuel Christian University (ECU)

**Emmanuel Christian University (ECU)** is one of South Sudan's developing premier Christian learning institutions, located **120 miles west of Juba** on the lush green lands of **Goli, Tore Payam, Yei River County, Central Equatoria State**.

ECU is an institution of **transformative learning** where academic excellence is combined with a **holistic development** of the learner based on the **Biblical perspective of life**.

- **Established**: 2001
- **Founded by**: Open Doors Africa Services in partnership with the **Evangelical Presbyterian Church (EPC)**
- **Accreditation**: Programs accredited by the **National Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Technology**
- **Language of Instruction**: English

### Vision
> *"A transformed people with academic excellence, rooted in the Word and glorifying God."*

### Mission
> *"To create a holistic church and society in South Sudan and beyond through transformative education, equipping godly men and women to develop sustainable communities."*

### Philosophy
> *"We provide quality education that can bring transformation to individuals and society; being sensitive to social, gender and disability needs and aspirations. We consider that all humans are equal and are capable of learning when exposed and guided to develop personal, God-given talents, which will help them to contribute positively to societal development."*

## 2. South Sudan Higher Education Framework

### National Education Structure: 8+4+4 System

| Level | Duration | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Primary** | 8 years | Basic education |
| **Secondary** | 4 years | Leads to South Sudan Certificate of Secondary Education (SSCSE) |
| **Tertiary / University** | 4–6 years | Undergraduate and postgraduate |

### Degree Program Duration by Field

| Field | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| Arts & Humanities | 4 years |
| Social Sciences | 4 years |
| Business Administration | 4 years |
| Education | 4 years |
| Science & Technology | 5 years |
| Engineering | 5 years |
| Medicine | 6 years |

### Degree Levels Offered in South Sudan

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Certificate** | Short-term foundational programs (1 year) |
| **Diploma** | Focused professional programs (2 years) |
| **Bachelor's Degree** | Full undergraduate degree (4 years) |
| **Master's Degree** | Postgraduate (1–2 years after Bachelor's) |
| **PhD / Doctoral** | Research-based (3+ years) |

### Regulatory Body
- **Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Technology** — oversees all public and private universities
- All admitted students' credentials must be submitted for verification and evaluation through the **Directorate of Admission, Evaluation and Authentication**

## 4. Admission System

### Admission Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **National Admission** | Candidates apply through the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Technology. Ministry processes applications and sends files to ECU. |
| **Direct Admission** | Students apply directly to ECU. ECU processes admission and sends certificates to the Ministry for verification, authentication, and national registration. |

### Admission Requirements

| Program Level | Requirements |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Bachelor (Degree)** | Must pass **7 out of the main subjects** of the South Sudan Certificate of Secondary Education (SSCSE) or its equivalent |
| **Diploma** | Must pass at least **4 subjects** of the main subjects of the SSCSE or its equivalent |
| **Certificate** | Basic secondary education completion |
| **Foreign Candidates** | Original certificate must be authenticated by the Ministries of Education, Foreign Affairs, and Embassy of South Sudan in the country where they studied |

### Post-Admission Process
- All admitted students' credentials are submitted to the **Office of the Registrar**
- The Registrar presents credentials for **verification and evaluation** at the **Directorate of Admission, Evaluation and Authentication** in the National Ministry of Higher Education

### Admission Pipeline (System Stages)
```
New → Interview → Admitted → Enrolled → (Active Student)
                → Rejected
```

## 5. Academic Structure & Semester System

### Semester System

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Academic Calendar** | Semester-based (2 main semesters per year) |
| **Semester Duration** | 17–18 weeks total |
| **Teaching Weeks** | ~14 weeks of lectures |
| **Reading/Review** | 1 week |
| **Examinations** | 2–3 weeks |
| **Recess Term** | Optional short term between main semesters |

### Academic Year Flow
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  SEMESTER 1 (Aug – Dec)                              │
│  14 wks teaching → 1 wk review → 2-3 wks exams      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  RECESS / BREAK                                      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  SEMESTER 2 (Jan – May)                              │
│  14 wks teaching → 1 wk review → 2-3 wks exams      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LONG VACATION (Jun – Jul)                           │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Student Academic Lifecycle
```
Admitted → Enrolled (Semester 1, Year 1)
         → Course Registration each semester
         → Assessments (Coursework + Exams)
         → Results / Transcripts per semester
         → Progression (Year 1 → Year 2 → ... → Final Year)
         → Graduated / Alumni
```

## 6. Grading & Credit Hour System

### Credit Unit (CU) System

| Instruction Type | Hours per Credit Unit |
|------------------|----------------------|
| **Lecture / Clinical** | 1 hour = 1 contact hour → 15 contact hrs = 1 CU |
| **Tutorial / Practical** | 2 hours = 1 contact hour |
| **Research / Fieldwork** | 4 hours = 1 contact hour |

### Grading Scale (South Sudan Standard)

| Letter Grade | Percentage | Grade Points | Classification |
|-------------|------------|-------------|----------------|
| **A** | 80% – 100% | **4.0** | Excellent |
| **B+** | 70% – 79% | **3.5** | Very Good |
| **B** | 60% – 69% | **3.0** | Good |
| **C+** | 55% – 59% | **2.5** | Fairly Good |
| **C** | 50% – 54% | **2.0** | Satisfactory Pass |
| **D** | 40% – 49% | **1.0** | Conditional Pass |
| **F** | Below 40% | **0.0** | Fail |

### CGPA Calculation
```
CGPA = Σ (Grade Points × Credit Units) / Σ (Total Credit Units Attempted)
```

### Degree Classification (based on CGPA)

| CGPA Range | Classification |
|-----------|----------------|
| 3.60 – 4.00 | First Class Honours |
| 3.00 – 3.59 | Second Class Upper Division |
| 2.50 – 2.99 | Second Class Lower Division |
| 2.00 – 2.49 | Pass |
| Below 2.00 | Fail / Discontinue |

### Assessment Weight Distribution (Typical)

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| **Coursework** (assignments, tests, practicals) | 30% – 40% |
| **Final Examination** | 60% – 70% |

## 10. New Modules Required

### 10.1 Course Registration Module
Students register for courses each semester based on their program and year of study.
- Course catalog browsing
- Add/drop courses within deadline
- Prerequisite validation
- Credit hour limit enforcement
- Advisor approval workflow

### 10.2 Transcript & Degree Audit
- Generate official academic transcripts
- Track completed vs required courses per program
- Calculate graduation eligibility
- Print-ready transcript in official format

### 10.3 Faculty & Department Management (Enhanced)
- Full CRUD for Faculties and Departments
- Assign Deans to Faculties
- Assign HODs to Departments
- Map Courses to Departments
- Map Programs to Departments

### 10.4 GPA/CGPA Engine
- Automatic grade point calculation per course
- Semester GPA computation
- Cumulative GPA across all semesters
- Degree classification assignment
- Academic standing determination (Good Standing, Probation, Dismissed)

### 10.5 Student Self-Service Portal
In a university context, students are responsible for their own records.
- **Academic Record**: View grades, results, and unofficial transcripts.
- **Course Selection**: Self-register for courses each semester.
- **Financial Status**: View real-time balance and update payment proof.
- **Personal Profile**: Update contact details and emergency contacts.
- **Digital ID**: View or print a student ID card profile.
- **Notifications**: Automated alerts for registration deadlines or fee reminders.

### 10.7 HOD-Controlled Marks Workflow
To maintain high academic standards, ECU implements a multi-stage marks approval process:
- **Department Course Ownership**: HODs manage the specific set of courses belonging to their department.
- **Lecturer Assignment**: HODs possess the sole authority to assign lecturers to courses within their department.
- **Marks Submission**: Lecturers submit marks in a **Draft** state. Once complete, they must submit them to the HOD for review (**Submitted** state).
- **HOD Review/Approval**: HODs can either **Approve** the marks (locking them for transcripts) or **Return** them to the lecturer for changes with comments.
- **Granular Unlock (Edit Grants)**: If an error is found post-submission, HODs can grant a **Single Student Edit Grant**. This allows the lecturer to fix only one specific student's mark, avoiding bulk accidental changes.
- **Audit Logging & Flagging**: Every change made after initial submission is **flagged** in the system with an "Edited" badge, and a full audit trail (Old Mark → New Mark) is visible to both HOD and Lecturer.
